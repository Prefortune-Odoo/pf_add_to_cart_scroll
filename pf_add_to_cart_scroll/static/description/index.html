Add to Cart Popup on Scroll

Odoo – Add to Cart Popup on Scroll helps businesses improve customer engagement and increase conversions in Odoo eCommerce. The module automatically displays an Add to Cart popup when customers scroll away from the purchase section on a product page, allowing them to quickly view product details, ratings, and pricing while accessing the Add to Cart button without scrolling back to the top.

Complete Workflow for Add to Cart Popup on Scroll

Improve your Add to Cart Popup on Scroll experience with a smart sticky purchase bar in Odoo eCommerce. The module automatically displays a customizable Add to Cart popup on scroll when customers scroll away from the original product purchase section.
